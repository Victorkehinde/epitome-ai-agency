First things first

We are not your competitor

We do not sell to end clients, and we do not run a competing agency in your market. We build. You sell. If your client never learns our name, we have done our job.

And we understand how you make money. Agencies typically work on a 20–40% margin on top of what their build partner charges. We price with that room left in it — so the number we quote you is your cost, not your ceiling.

What we build for your clients

Why agencies work with us

The reasons that actually matter

We are in your working day

We are based in Lagos, Nigeria — UTC+1. That is the same clock as London, one hour from Berlin and Paris, six from New York. Your 9am is our 3pm: you send a question in the morning and it is answered before you close. Teams six to twelve hours ahead cost you a day on every single exchange.

Fixed price, scoped in writing

You get a written scope and a fixed price before we start. That means you can quote your client with confidence instead of padding for the unknown — and it means nobody discovers a moving number halfway through the build.

You own everything

Your client, your contract, your brand, your margin. Developer accounts are registered in your client's name, never ours. On final payment your client owns the code and the assets.

We never touch your client

All communication goes through you. We will sign an NDA before we see a brief. We do not add your client to a list, and we do not approach them — not during the project, and not afterwards.

Capacity that appears when you need it

Overflow work, a second build running alongside your own team, or a service line you have been quietly turning down because there was nobody free to deliver it. Saying no to those projects is a slow revenue leak.

English, first language

Business English, spoken and written, not translated. Your brief is read the way you wrote it, and your client never sees a clumsy email with your logo on it.

How a project runs

Brief to handover in five steps

1

You send the brief

A scope sheet, a rough idea, or the notes your client already sent you. We will sign an NDA first if you want one.

2

We scope and price it

A written scope and a fixed price, usually back to you within two working days.

3

You approve — and mark it up

Approve it, or send it straight to your client with your own margin added on top.

4

We build, you stay the contact

You remain your client's point of contact throughout. We give you progress updates you can forward without editing.

5

Handover

Working product, source code, all credentials, and a written handover note. Nothing held back, nothing hostage.
