What it does

It sits in front of the inbox you already have. Nothing about your portal subscriptions changes.

  1. Picks up the enquiryThe emails Rightmove, Zoopla, OnTheMarket and PrimeLocation already send your office, plus enquiries that arrive by email.
  2. Reads it properlyThe property, the budget, the move-in timing, and whether they are ready to move or just browsing.
  3. Replies within a minuteWith your available viewing slots and a link to choose one. In your voice, with wording you approve.
  4. Lets the tenant book themselvesNo phone tag, no "we'll get back to you", no double bookings.
  5. Follows up if they go quietOn a fixed rhythm, on your behalf, until they book or clearly aren't interested.
  6. Shows you one queueWho's booked, who's warm, who needs a human. Nothing hiding in an inbox.

Your agency stays the data controller. EnquiryDesk is the processor. We handle the enquiry on your instruction and for no other purpose.

Enquiries and their replies are stored in our database, hosted in the London region, and are never sold, shared or used to contact anyone except the person who made the enquiry — on your behalf.

Ask us to delete an agency's data at any time and we remove it. Full written detail is available on request before you start a pilot.
